How do I find the best baby name with meaning?

Start by searching a name, meaning, origin, religion or theme. NameVerse connects each query to curated name lists, cultural context and related browsing paths so you can compare options quickly.

Which baby names are trending in 2026?

Parents are searching for short, meaningful and globally pronounceable names. Popular homepage picks include Muhammad, Aisha, Rayan, Noah, Olivia, Aarav, Diya and Ananya, with more options in the trending names hub.

Can I search names by religion, origin or meaning?

Yes. Browse Islamic, Hindu and Christian names by religion, then narrow by origins such as Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Sanskrit, Hebrew, Greek or Latin. You can also search meanings such as light, love, strength or peace.

Are NameVerse meanings verified?

NameVerse prioritizes verified meanings and cultural context. Entries are reviewed for linguistic origin, common usage and faith-aware interpretation before being surfaced in search and curated lists.

Is NameVerse free to use?

Yes. NameVerse search, name meanings, browsing pages, FAQs and name discovery tools are free for parents, families and researchers.
